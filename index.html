Physics notes
Each wavelength from 400 to 700 nm is traced independently through a triangular prism. Refraction uses Snell’s law; transmission uses unpolarized Fresnel coefficients. Strong refraction may cause total internal reflection, which is a real optical effect. Wavelength colors are display approximations rather than colorimetrically calibrated.
